Smaller is not monotonically better:8 and32 both worsen the conflict and
issue-wait metrics relative to16. Restore16 before further experimentation.
The metrics do not prove every latency difference causal: occupancy is
waves/CU, issue-wait is in four-wave-cycle units rather than stall percent,
and LDSBankConflict uses the installed gfx950 formula, not conflicts/access.
Scheduler eligibility, achieved bandwidth, actual overlap and full stalls
remain unmeasured. Next inspect LDS read scheduling/operand register
prefetch rather than making another unsupported padding assumption.
