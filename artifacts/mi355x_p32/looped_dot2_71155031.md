## Math, SSA, and dependency investigation

The retained GEMV reduces each K tile and then accumulates scalar tile sums.
The experiment retains FP32 vector partials across tiles and reduces once:
`sum_pair sum_tile (w_even*x_even + w_odd*x_odd)`.
Every K term occurs once, without power-of-two padding, for the tested K values.
This is real-arithmetic equivalence with changed FP32 evaluation order, not a
bitwise-equivalence claim. Packed FP16 operands feed FP32 dot accumulators;
the residual specialization instead adds high/low weights in FP32 before
FP32 multiplication and accumulation. Output narrowing remains unchanged.

The initial no-delay Triton and Gluon kernels both corrupted results. On a
K5120 all-ones diagnostic some outputs were 4992 rather than 5120; K6144
similarly lost 128. Explicit layout alone therefore did not fix correctness.
Adding `s_nop 7` after each inline dot fixed both diagnostic cases. The tests
repeat these cases ten times and also exercise FP16 subnormal inputs with
exact power-of-two expected results. The conservative delay is a measured
workaround, not proof of the precise hardware hazard or minimum required wait.
The rejected no-delay Gluon report preserves its failing results and source hash.

SSA retains a single reduction after the loop. Triton still lowers activation
broadcast through LDS and keeps a partially unrolled loop. Gluon assigns a
wave to an output row, eliminates LDS broadcast, and fully unrolls K at the
tested sizes. The final Gluon ISA has 40/48 dot instructions for K5120/6144,
respectively, and 52/59 total static NOP instructions including conservative
inline waits and compiler-inserted waits. Address bases are reused, but larger
offsets still generate 64-bit address-add pairs. Non-residual constexpr
specializations contain no residual loads. There is no padded-K mask stream.

Gluon removes 76.9% of issued VALU and all issued LDS on gate/up, but end-to-end

host overhead remain possible limitations; their shares were not measured here.

function bodies; loop bodies are not multiplied by dynamic iterations.
Occupancy, bank conflicts, scheduler eligibility, stall reasons, and achieved
bandwidth were not collected; no claims are made for those metrics.

## Next decision

Do not promote either broad M1 dispatch. Keep AITER as the stronger existing
small-M prototype. Prioritize measuring/removing the large-M attention correction
copy and gate/up correction overhead, preserving FP32 correction, stream/graph
behavior, output ownership, and autograd fallback. A minimum-delay study remains
a bounded secondary experiment, not grounds to remove validated waits speculatively.
