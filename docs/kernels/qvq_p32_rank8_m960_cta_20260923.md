# H100 M960 Rank-8 Hadamard CTA geometry (2026-09-23 22:21 UTC)

This experiment starts from merged QVQ `1096750e93d9e417194805845d8e0f33c183f0db`
and merged ZML M960 auto-dispatch. The goal remains **2x B128 useful prefill
throughput** against the original ~27,023 tok/s baseline; this change is a
small, separately measured increment, not completion of that goal.

## Prefill-only trace and hypothesis

On the rank-8-enabled Llama 3.2 1B F6/P32 seed-7 snapshot, one real M960
request spent about 5.50 ms in W3 compressed P32 kernels, 1.99 ms in 48
wide rank-8 Hadamard epilogues, 1.41 ms in their projectors, and 2.05 ms in
FA2 attention (Nsight Systems GPU kernel durations, not server wall time).
The 1,024-thread, one-row epilogue used 32 warps and a full-CTA barrier for
the cross-warp Hadamard stages. A smaller CTA might reduce synchronization
cost without changing the per-element FP16 arithmetic. The initially
suspected 8.46 ms dense GEMM was **not** a prefill hotspot: HLO identified
it as the vocabulary head, with 31 of 32 launches belonging to decode.

## Geometry and local oracle

The production selector uses 512 threads only when M=960, rank count=8, and
N is 2,048 or 8,192. Other shapes keep their previous thread count. No
environment flag, dense-weight cache, mathematical reorder, or new workspace
is involved. A diagnostic-only environment sweep was removed before the
final build. The existing CUDA smoke compared the fused and separate paths
bit-for-bit at M960, K2048, N2048/8192, with 24 repeated launches in three
alternating-order CUDA-event rounds. The standard rank-8 FP16 oracle also
passed all its N=16–8192 cases.

| M960 projector + epilogue | 1,024 threads | 512 threads | 256 threads |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| N2048, ms/call | 0.03154–0.03169 | 0.02894–0.02938 | 0.02923 |
| N8192, ms/call | 0.10027–0.10044 | 0.09681–0.09728 | 0.10635 |

The 512-thread N2048/N8192 pair wins repeatedly; 256 loses on N8192.
The production-shaped no-flag build measured 0.02919 and 0.09728 ms/call,
respectively, while preserving bitwise output. Nsight Systems confirmed
48 actual M960 rank-8 Hadamard launches at `grid.x=960`, `block.x=512`,
averaging 39.10 microseconds of GPU kernel time each versus 41.44 microseconds
in the earlier 1,024-thread trace. Profiler binaries stay local.

## Full B128 GSM8K-Platinum gate

Both arms used the **same optimized ZML runner built against this QVQ code**;
only the temporary diagnostic CTA control changed. Model, ordered 1,209
requests, reference/dataset hashes, B128/M960, FA2, logical context 8,192,
544 physical KV pages, rank-8 enabled, GPU-local CPU pin, one-row warmup,
and scoring were identical. After the sweep, a separate no-flag rebuild
selected 512 threads automatically and repeated the full gate.

The matched prefill gain is 0.30–0.40%, with zero invalid samples and no
observed decode regression in this band. The full JSON, local one-row trace,
and benchmark artifacts are under

not committed. This result does not justify stopping work on the much larger
